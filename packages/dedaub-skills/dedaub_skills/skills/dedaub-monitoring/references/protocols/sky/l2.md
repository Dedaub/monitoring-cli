# Sky SkyLink (bridged USDS / sUSDS) — Topics, Selectors, Addresses (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Avalanche, +Unichain)

**Status:** verified against live RPC on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum One, Optimism, Avalanche C-Chain and Unichain, the on-chain ChainLog, the `sparkdotfi/spark-address-registry` chain files and Sourcify-verified ABIs on 2026-10-30.
**Scope:** SkyLink, the Sky-owned bridges that carry USDS and sUSDS off Ethereum: the OP-stack token bridge (Base 8453, Optimism 10, Unichain 130), the Arbitrum custom gateway (Arbitrum One 42161), the LayerZero OFT path (Avalanche 43114, plus a Solana peer), the escrows and governance relays on both sides, the legacy Maker DAI bridges (Optimism, Arbitrum), and the cross-chain sUSDS rate oracles. Topics and selectors are chain-agnostic. Addresses are network-specific. **BNB (56), Polygon PoS (137), Robinhood Chain (4663) and Arc (5042) have no SkyLink deployment, no USDS, no sUSDS and no SKY** (`eth_getCode` = `0x` at every Sky literal, and no OFT peer for their LayerZero endpoint IDs).

SkyLink has two designs. **Native rollup bridges** (Base, Optimism, Unichain, Arbitrum) **lock** USDS/sUSDS in an Ethereum `Escrow` and **mint** a Sky-owned L2 token. The L2 token is the `Usds` code behind an ERC-1967 UUPS proxy, including for sUSDS: on L2, sUSDS is a plain ERC-20 that tracks the Ethereum share, not an ERC-4626 vault. **LayerZero OFT** (Avalanche) locks USDS/sUSDS in a `SkyOFTAdapter` on Ethereum and mints through a `SkyOFTAdapterMintBurn` on the remote chain. Governance reaches every L2 through a per-chain `L1GovernanceRelay` → `L2GovernanceRelay` pair, which is a ward on the L2 bridge and tokens.

All L1 and L2 bridge contracts are UUPS proxies. All escrows, relays and OFT adapters are immutable.

## 11. Detection invariants & gotchas

1. **sUSDS on L2 is not a vault.** It has no `deposit`/`Drip`. Its USD value = balance × the L2 `SSRAuthOracle` conversion rate. Yield accrues only on the Ethereum vault that backs the escrow.
2. **L1 supply is locked, not burned.** Native bridges lock in the escrow. OFT adapters lock in the adapter itself. L2 supply ≈ escrow (or adapter) balance on Ethereum. A drift is an alert.
3. **Shared escrows.** `OPTIMISM_ESCROW` and `ARBITRUM_ESCROW` also hold legacy DAI. Separate by token.
4. **`0x10E6593C…` is three contracts.** Ethereum: legacy OP `L1DAITokenBridge`. Optimism and Arbitrum: `L2GovernanceRelay`. `0x46719477…` is the OP escrow on Ethereum and the legacy DAI bridge/gateway on Optimism/Arbitrum. Always key on `(chainId, address)`.
5. **Withdraw limits.** L2 bridges enforce `maxWithdraws(token)` per transaction (`MaxWithdrawSet`). OFT adapters enforce inbound/outbound rate limits (`RateLimitsChanged`). The Avalanche path launched with $5 M/day.
6. **`close()` is one-way.** `Closed()` on a bridge stops new deposits. In-flight messages still finalize.
7. **Standard-bridge topic collisions.** OP `ERC20BridgeInitiated`/`Finalized` and Arbitrum `DepositInitiated`/`WithdrawalFinalized` are shared with canonical bridges. OFT `OFTSent`/`OFTReceived` are shared with every LayerZero OFT. Filter on the Sky addresses.
8. **Two `DepositFinalized` and two `WithdrawalInitiated`.** The OP legacy `L2DAITokenBridge` versions carry `(l1Token, l2Token, from, to, amount, data)`. The Arbitrum gateway versions carry `(l1Token, from, to, amount)` and `(l1Token, from, to, l2ToL1Id, exitNum, amount)`. The topic0 values differ.
9. **SKY is not bridged.** The Base SKY contract has zero supply and no bridge mapping (`l1ToL2Token(SKY)` = `0x0` on every L1 bridge). SPK and GROVE use their own LayerZero OFTs (Spark/Grove docs).
10. **Solana is a USDS OFT peer** (EID 30168). Solana flows appear on Ethereum as `OFTSent`/`OFTReceived` with `dstEid`/`srcEid` = 30168.

## 13. Verification & sources

- **Topic0 / selectors:** recomputed locally as `keccak256(signature)` from the Sourcify-verified ABIs (implementation ABIs for proxies). - **Addresses:** ChainLog keys on Ethereum; L2 addresses from `sparkdotfi/spark-address-registry` (`Base.sol`, `Optimism.sol`, `Arbitrum.sol`, `Unichain.sol`), the Sky developer guide (Base), and `sky-ecosystem/sky-protocol-info` (Avalanche). Avalanche token addresses read live from `SkyOFTAdapterMintBurn.token()`. Every L1 bridge `l1ToL2Token(USDS/sUSDS)` and every L2 bridge `l1ToL2Token(USDS/sUSDS)` read live and matched. OFT peers read with `peers(eid)` for EIDs 30100–30499: USDS → 30106 (Avalanche) + 30168 (Solana), sUSDS → 30106 only. SSR forwarder targets read with `l2Oracle()`. All existence-checked via `eth_getCode`; proxy implementations from the EIP-1967 slot.
- **Chain coverage:** every token, bridge and adapter literal checked on all nine target chains plus Unichain. BNB, Polygon, Robinhood Chain and Arc return `0x` for all of them.
