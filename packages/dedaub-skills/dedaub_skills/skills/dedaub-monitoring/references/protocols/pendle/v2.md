# Pendle V2 — Topics, Selectors, Addresses (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, BNB, Optimism, Robinhood Chain)

**Status:** verified against live RPC on every listed chain and the canonical `pendle-finance/pendle-core-v2-public` repo (`deployments/<chainId>-core.json`, npm `@pendle/core-v2@6.12.0` ABIs) on 2026-10-30.
**Scope:** the Pendle V2 yield-tokenization core: the Router (`PendleRouterV4` + action facets), the market factories (V1, V3, V4, V5, V6/V7), the yield-contract (PT/YT) factories, the SY factory, the per-market AMM (`PendleMarket`), PT, YT and SY tokens, the Limit Router, and the PT/YT/LP oracle. Chains: Ethereum (1), Arbitrum One (42161), Base (8453), BNB Smart Chain (56), Optimism (10), Robinhood Chain (4663). Avalanche (43114) carries only cross-chain PT periphery (see [governance.md](governance.md)). **Not deployed on Polygon PoS (137) or Arc (5042).** Topics and selectors are chain-agnostic. Addresses are network-specific.

Pendle splits a yield-bearing asset into two tokens. **SY** (Standardized Yield) wraps the asset. A **YT contract** takes SY and mints equal amounts of **PT** (principal, redeemable 1:1 at expiry) and **YT** (all yield until expiry). Each `(SY, expiry)` pair has one PT and one YT. Each PT has one or more **markets** (`PendleMarket`, also the LP token "PLP"). A market is a time-decaying AMM that trades PT against SY.

Markets and PT/YT pairs are **immutable clones**. They come from factories. A new factory generation does not upgrade old markets. Old markets stay live until expiry and keep their factory. Index **every** factory generation on a chain to see every market. The newest factories (`marketFactoryV6`, `yieldContractFactoryV6`) are **TransparentUpgradeableProxy** contracts. On Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, BNB and Robinhood Chain the `marketFactoryV6` proxy now runs `PendleMarketFactoryV7Upg` (`VERSION() = 7`). On Optimism it still answers `VERSION() = 6`.

The Router `0x888888888889758f76e7103c6cbf23abbf58f946` is the same vanity address on all six chains. It is a small (287 B) **selector-to-facet dispatcher** (Diamond-like, not EIP-2535). It `delegatecall`s each selector to a facet that `selectorToFacet(bytes4)` returns. Facets rotate without an `Upgraded` event: the router emits `SelectorToFacetSet(bytes4,address)` instead.

## 9. Detection invariants & gotchas

1. **Index every factory generation.** A V3 market emits through its own address forever. Filter markets by "created by any Pendle factory on this chain", not by the newest factory.
2. **Two `CreateNewMarket` topic0s.** V1 uses the 4-arg form `0x166ae5f5…`. V3+ use the 5-arg form `0xae811fae…`. A query on one topic misses the other generation.
3. **`marketFactoryV6` runs V7 code** on ETH, Arb, Base, BNB and Robinhood. The registry key says "V6", `VERSION()` says 7, and markets it creates say `VERSION()=7`. Events did not change.
4. **The router is the `caller`, not the user.** Market `Swap.caller` is usually `0x8888…f946`. The real user is the router event `caller` (topic1) or the tx sender. Join the market `Swap` and the router `SwapPtAndToken`/`SwapYtAndToken` by tx hash.
5. **Signed amounts.** Market `Swap.netPtOut` / `netSyOut` and router `netPtToAccount` / `netYtToAccount` / `netTokenToAccount` are `int256`. Positive = to the account. A PT buy has `netPtOut > 0`, `netSyOut < 0`.
6. **YT swaps go through the PT market.** There is no YT pool. A YT buy shows as a market `Swap` (PT sold) plus a YT `Mint`. Use the router `SwapYtAndToken` event to label it.
7. **`RedeemRewards` topic0 is shared** by markets, YTs and gauges (`0x78d61a0c…`). Disambiguate by emitter type.
8. **`Mint`/`Burn` on YT differ from `Mint`/`Burn` on market.** Different signatures, different topic0s (YT `0xc0025304…`/`0x5d624aa9…`; market `0xb4c03061…`/`0x4cf25bc1…`).
9. **Facet rotation is silent at the proxy level.** The JSON registry facets (`0xd8d200d9…` etc.) are **not** the live facets on ETH, Arb, Base, BNB and Robinhood (verified with `selectorToFacet`). Only Optimism still routes to the JSON facets. Watch `SelectorToFacetSet` from the router.
10. **Same address, different role per chain.** The deployer reused nonces: `0x17f100fb…3d58` is the OP V1 market factory **and** the Base gauge controller. `0xf5a7de2d…ddc8` is the Arb V1 market factory **and** the OP V1 yield factory. `0x704478dd…34d0` is the OP RouterStatic **and** the BNB gauge controller. Always key on `(chainId, address)`.
11. **Registry entries can be stale.** On Optimism, `pendleSwap` and `lpWrapperFactory` addresses return `0x`. Existence-check before you alert.
12. **Expiry.** After `expiry()`, a market rejects swaps. Exits use `exitPostExpTo*` and YT `redeemPY` with PT only. A spike of `ExitPostExp*` after an expiry is normal.
13. **SY events are not uniform.** `Deposit`/`Redeem`/`ClaimRewards` are the common interface. Each SY can add its own events (Robinhood SY `0x346ae4fc…` emits 4 extra topics).
