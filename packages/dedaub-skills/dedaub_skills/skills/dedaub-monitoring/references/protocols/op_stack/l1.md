# OP Stack native bridges, settlement side — Topics, Selectors, Addresses (Ethereum + BNB Smart Chain; L3 settlement on Base is in l2.md)

**Status:** verified on 2026-09-29 against live RPC on all eight target chains (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum One, Optimism, Polygon PoS, BNB Smart Chain, Avalanche C-Chain, Robinhood Chain), the Superchain Registry (`ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry` at commit `2507d80d5eee7b1e0c8aa1485489936be62b7fd1`, 2026-09-28, plus the parent commits of the removals of Base, Arena-Z, Swellchain and SnaxChain), the `ethereum-optimism/optimism` contracts (`packages/contracts-bedrock`, ABI snapshots at commit `54e770f157ee975592f2857c885ec831e10a0e68` and the tags `op-contracts/v1.8.0`, `v4.0.0`, `v5.0.0`, `v6.0.0`), `mantlenetworkio/mantle-v2`, `MetisProtocol/mvm`, and the L2BEAT project discovery files.
**Scope:** the settlement-layer contracts of every mainnet chain of the Superchain Registry and of the other OP Stack chains with bridge activity on Ethereum: 49 chains on Ethereum (31 registry chains, 4 chains that left the registry, 11 chains outside the registry, 1 fork with its own events, 2 unidentified deployments), Metis (an OVM-era fork with no portal), an unofficial deployment that uses GIWA's chain id, 5 token-specific bridges that reuse the OP Stack event names, and opBNB plus one unidentified chain on BNB Smart Chain. The L2 predeploys on Optimism and Base and the OP Stack L3 contracts on Base are in [l2.md](l2.md). Blast is in [`../blast_native/`](../blast_native/core.md). Topics and selectors are chain-agnostic; addresses are network-specific.

An OP Stack chain keeps its bridge on its settlement layer. Each chain has its own proxies: an **OptimismPortal** (the ETH or gas-token escrow, and the only contract that creates L2 deposit transactions), an **L1StandardBridge** (the ERC-20 escrow), an **L1CrossDomainMessenger** (the message layer that the bridges use), an **L1ERC721Bridge**, a **SystemConfig**, a proof contract (a **DisputeGameFactory** for `OptimismPortal2`, an **L2OutputOracle** for older portals), a **ProxyAdmin**, and on four chains an **ETHLockbox** that holds the ETH in place of the portal. The event signatures are the same on every chain. Only the addresses change.

Every deposit ends in exactly one `TransactionDeposited` at the portal: a bridge deposit, a messenger message and a plain ETH send to the portal. The bridge and messenger events in the same transaction name the token, the user and the amount. Every withdrawal ends in `WithdrawalFinalized` at the portal, days after `WithdrawalProven`, with the payout events of the bridge in the same transaction as the finalization.

Three facts to know before indexing:

1. **The topic0 values collide.** All OP Stack chains, Blast, Mantle, Metis, the Lido token bridges, Scroll's `L1ScrollMessenger` and Morph's `L1CrossDomainMessenger` emit some of the same topic0 values on Ethereum. Key every rule on `(chain, emitter address)`, never on topic0 alone.
2. **The registry is not the whole population.** Base left the Superchain Registry on 2026-04-27, Arena-Z and Swellchain on 2026-05-26, SnaxChain on 2025-11-17. Eleven active chains were never in it. An unofficial deployment uses GIWA's chain id 9134 (§3.5). The forks and variants are in §3.8.
3. **An upgrade does not always emit `Upgraded`.** The portal, SystemConfig, lockbox and proof contracts are EIP-1967 proxies that emit `Upgraded`. The L1CrossDomainMessenger is a `ResolvedDelegateProxy`: its upgrade is an `AddressSet` on the AddressManager. The L1StandardBridge is an `L1ChugSplashProxy`: its upgrade emits no event.

| Contract | Role | Proxy | Events that matter |
|---|---|---|---|

**Portal generations** (per-chain versions in §3.2): `OptimismPortal` 1.x reads an L2OutputOracle and pauses by itself (Mantle, Manta Pacific, opBNB). `OptimismPortal` 2.x reads an L2OutputOracle and pauses through SuperchainConfig; 2.8.x adds `depositERC20Transaction` for custom-gas-token chains. `OptimismPortal2` 3.x and later reads a DisputeGameFactory, emits `WithdrawalProvenExtension1` and has `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof`. Versions 4.x and later can route ETH to an ETHLockbox.

**Decode `TransactionDeposited`.** `from` (topic1) is the L1 sender. When the caller is a contract, the portal applies the L1-to-L2 alias: `from = caller + 0x1111000000000000000000000000000000001111` (mod 2^160). For a bridge or messenger deposit, `from` is the aliased L1CrossDomainMessenger and `to` (topic2) is `0x4200000000000000000000000000000000000007`; the user is in the bridge event. `version` (topic3) selects the layout of `opaqueData`, which is an ABI-encoded `bytes` (offset, length, then the packed bytes):

| `version` | Packed layout of `opaqueData` (byte offsets) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `mint` uint256 [0..31], `value` uint256 [32..63], `gasLimit` uint64 [64..71], `isCreation` bool [72], `data` [73..] | Every portal except Mantle. `mint` is the ETH that arrives on L2. On a custom-gas-token portal that uses `depositERC20Transaction` (§3.2), `mint` is the gas token pulled into the portal, not ETH. |
| 1 | `mntValue` uint256 [0..31], `mntTxValue` uint256 [32..63], `ethValue` uint256 [64..95] (= `msg.value`), `ethTxValue` uint256 [96..127], `gasLimit` uint64 [128..135], `isCreation` bool [136], `data` [137..] | Mantle only. A version-0 decoder reads MNT as ETH. |

### 1.9 What fires together (one transaction, in log order; verified on the sample transactions of §11)

| Action | Events in order | Value movement in the same transaction |
|---|---|---|
| ETH deposit through the bridge (`bridgeETH`, `depositETH`) | L1StandardBridge `ETHDepositInitiated` + `ETHBridgeInitiated` → ETHLockbox `ETHLocked` (lockbox chains only) → portal `TransactionDeposited` → messenger `SentMessage` + `SentMessageExtension1` | `msg.value` moves user → bridge → messenger → portal (→ lockbox) as internal calls. No ERC-20 row. |
| ERC-20 deposit (`bridgeERC20`, `depositERC20`) | token `Transfer` user → bridge (escrow) → bridge `ERC20DepositInitiated` + `ERC20BridgeInitiated` → portal `TransactionDeposited` → messenger `SentMessage` + `SentMessageExtension1` | L1-native token: escrowed in the L1StandardBridge. L2-native token: burned (`Transfer` to the zero address). |
| Direct deposit to the portal (`depositTransaction`, or ETH sent to the portal) | portal `TransactionDeposited` only (+ `ETHLocked` on lockbox chains) | `msg.value` into the portal. `from` = `to` = the sender for an EOA self-deposit. |
| Withdrawal proof | portal `WithdrawalProven` + `WithdrawalProvenExtension1` | None. Any account can prove; several proofs of one withdrawal can exist. |
| ETH withdrawal finalization | ETHLockbox `ETHUnlocked` (lockbox chains) → bridge `ETHWithdrawalFinalized` + `ETHBridgeFinalized` → messenger `RelayedMessage` → portal `WithdrawalFinalized` | ETH moves (lockbox →) portal → messenger → bridge → recipient as internal calls. |
| ERC-20 withdrawal finalization | token `Transfer` bridge → recipient → bridge `ERC20WithdrawalFinalized` + `ERC20BridgeFinalized` → messenger `RelayedMessage` → portal `WithdrawalFinalized` | The escrow pays the recipient. The caller is usually a relayer, not the user. |
| Mantle MNT deposit | MNT `Transfer` user → bridge, bridge → messenger, messenger → portal → `MNTDepositInitiated` + `MNTBridgeInitiated` → `TransactionDeposited` (version 1) → `SentMessage` + `SentMessageExtension1(sender, mntValue, ethValue)` | Three MNT `Transfer` rows for one deposit. The portal holds the MNT. Count one row. |
| Metis ERC-20 deposit | token `Transfer` user → bridge → `TransactionEnqueued` → Metis `SentMessage` (with `chainId`) → `ERC20ChainID` → `ERC20DepositInitiated` | The L1StandardBridge escrows the token. METIS has `l2Token` = `0xDeadDeAddeAddEAddeadDEaDDEAdDeaDDeAD0000`. |

## 9. Detection invariants & gotchas

1. **Key on the emitter.** The same topic0 comes from 49 portals, 49 bridges and 49 messengers on Ethereum, plus Blast, Mantle, Metis, the Lido bridges (`ERC20DepositInitiated`), Scroll's `L1ScrollMessenger` `0x6774Bcbd5ceCeF1336b5300fb5186a12DDD8b367` and Morph's `L1CrossDomainMessenger` `0xDc71366EFFA760804DCFC3EDF87fa2A6f1623304` (`RelayedMessage`: 157 and 38 of the 298 in the window), and at least 18 more OP Stack proof contracts (§3.7). On Base, the L2 predeploys and the L3 settlement contracts emit the same topic0 values (l2.md).
2. **Count one record per transfer.** Decode one event of each legacy/new pair (`ETHDepositInitiated`/`ETHBridgeInitiated`, `ERC20DepositInitiated`/`ERC20BridgeInitiated` and the finalization pairs). In the window: 79 and 79, 110 and 107 (the 3 extra `ERC20DepositInitiated` are Metis, which has no new event).
3. **`TransactionDeposited` covers every path; the bridge events do not.** 443 portal deposits against 79 ETH and 110 ERC-20 bridge deposits on Ethereum. A direct `depositTransaction` or a plain ETH send to the portal emits only `TransactionDeposited`. Read ETH from `mint` (version 0). Most deposits carry no ETH: filter `mint > 0`.
4. **The user is not `TransactionDeposited.from` for a bridge deposit.** `from` is the aliased messenger (for example `0x977F82a600a1414E583f7F13623F1ac5D58B1C0b` for Base, `0x36BDE71C97B33Cc4729cf772aE268934f7AB70B2` for OP Mainnet). The user is `from` in the bridge event. A contract that calls the portal directly also appears aliased (`+ 0x1111000000000000000000000000000000001111`). The `to` of a bridge event is never aliased.
5. **Where the ETH is.** On OP Mainnet, Ink, Soneium and Unichain, the ETH of a deposit moves on to the ETHLockbox in the same transaction (`ETHLocked`); the OP Mainnet portal held 0 ETH and its lockbox 177,805.24 ETH on 2026-09-29. On the other chains the portal holds the ETH (Base portal: 776,836.66 ETH). On custom-gas-token chains the portal holds the gas token, and `mint` counts that token. On Mantle the portal holds ETH and MNT.
6. **Link keys.** Deposit: the L2 transaction hash is computable from Ethereum data alone (l2.md §6): `sourceHash = keccak256(bytes32(0) ‖ keccak256(l1BlockHash ‖ uint256(logIndex)))`, where `logIndex` is the index of the `TransactionDeposited` log in its block. Second deposit key: `msgHash` of §1.3 = `RelayedMessage.msgHash` on L2 (bridge deposits only). Withdrawal: `withdrawalHash = keccak256(abi.encode(nonce, sender, target, value, gasLimit, data))` = `MessagePassed.withdrawalHash` on L2 = topic1 of `WithdrawalProven` and `WithdrawalFinalized`. All three keys are on chain on both sides.
7. **Proof is not payout.** Count `WithdrawalFinalized` with `success = true`. More than one party can prove the same withdrawal (`WithdrawalProvenExtension1` names each prover). A failed relay (`FailedRelayedMessage`) leaves the value in the messenger until a replay.
8. **Mantle and Metis are different.** Mantle uses `version` = 1 opaque data, MNT events and its own portal selectors (§1.7, §2.1). Metis has no portal: key deposits on `TransactionEnqueued._queueIndex` and read ETH from the Metis `ETHDepositInitiated` with `chainId`.
9. **`SystemConfig.batchInbox()` can be wrong.** On 12 chains it returns `0x00 ‖ keccak256(uint256(chainId))[0:19]`, a default that no batcher uses: BOB, Lyra Chain, Metal L2, Mode, Orderly, Shape, Superseed, World Chain, Zora, Aevo, Phala and Zircuit. The registry's `batch_inbox_addr` and the address that receives the batcher's transactions agree; §3.1 lists that address. MegaETH's SystemConfig names an old inbox contract (`0x02B8d1329B653d6f53A8420C8DDbBbb5518F51b2`, last batch on 2026-04-14).
